# Code of conduct

- Talk about the code, not about the people who wrote it.
- No insults, harassment, or attacks on anyone for who they are.
- Disagree with reasons. "No" is a fine answer to a proposal; say why.
- Do not post other people's private information.

The maintainer removes what breaks these rules and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report something, open an issue or mention [@azabroflovski](https://github.com/azabroflovski). For
something you would rather not post in public, write to the maintainer privately through
[a private report](https://github.com/azabroflovski/oyna-ui/security/advisories/new), or use
**Report content** in a comment's menu to reach GitHub.
