# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Tokens, the base stylesheet and the generated background.
- Hotkeys read from physical keys: `useHotkey`, and the `hotkey` prop of Button and Toggle.
- Components: Background, Surface, Card, Button, Kbd, Badge, Input, Field, Toggle, Tabs, Select,
  KeyCapture, Dialog, Tooltip, Toaster, Stat, Progress, Pips, Sparkline, BarChart, Table.
